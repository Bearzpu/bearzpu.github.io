Bearzpu apps

A small set of personal web apps, made and run by one person. They aren't open to the public: each is used by its owner, or by a small team the owner invites.

The apps

  • Unmapped Streets: lets a delivery team record streets and driveways that standard map apps don't know about, so drivers can find them on the day.
  • Drift: a personal to-do list and journal.
  • Bear-z-Pod: a personal podcast player.

Some of the apps let you sign in with Google. That is used to keep your data tied to your account and, where an app offers it, to save backups and photos in a folder in your own Google Drive.
