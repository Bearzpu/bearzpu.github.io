Privacy policy

This covers the small personal apps listed on the home page (Unmapped Streets, Drift and Bear-z-Pod). They are small personal projects, shared with a limited number of testers rather than offered through an open public sign-up. This page explains what they store and why.

What we store

  • Your sign-in email, so you can log in and your data stays tied to your account.
  • The data you add to an app, for example street records, tasks, journal entries, or podcast shows and listening history. Much of it lives only on your own device; some apps also sync it to a database so it appears on your other devices.
  • A Google Drive access token (a "refresh token"), where an app saves to Drive. It is kept on our server so the app can save and read your files without asking you to sign in each time.

Google Drive

Where an app uses Google Drive, it asks only for the drive.file permission. That lets the app see and change only the files and folders it created itself, such as photos or backup copies of your own data. It can't see, read or change anything else in your Drive.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers or used for anything other than running the apps. Your data isn't shared with other people unless you choose to share it inside an app. In Unmapped Streets, photos are viewable by anyone who has their link, so the app can display them.

Removing your data

You can sign out in each app's settings. You can also remove an app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete any folders the app made in your Drive. To have anything stored on our server deleted as well, ask the owner below and your account and anything you've added will be deleted.

Drift

Drift is the to-do app. This section covers what it stores beyond the general points above.

  • What it stores: your sign-in email; everything you add in Drift (tasks, journal, health and mood entries, lists and settings, including any API keys you type into Settings); notification tokens, so it can remind you while Drift is closed; and the Google Drive refresh token used for backups and photos (see Google Drive above).
  • Where and how: on your phone, and synced to a Supabase database over HTTPS. Supabase encrypts stored data (AES-256, see supabase.com/security). Every request is checked against the signed-in account, and the database only lets an account reach its own rows.
  • Who can see it: as the database owner, the developer can technically see the stored data. It is never looked at, sold, shared or used for anything except running Drift.
  • Shared tasks: people you share a task with see that task.
  • How long: kept until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Reach out: if you use Reach out, Drift reads your contacts on the phone only to show the list; only the person you pick is saved with the task.

Sleep (Android app)

If you switch on the Sleep page in the Android app and allow it, Drift reads sleep sessions from Health Connect. It only reads and never writes. It reads each night's start and end, the sleep stages if your sleep app records them, and which app recorded it. Nights are stored and synced like the rest of your Drift data, and are used only to show your sleep and shape your My day schedule and the list. Sleep data is not used for advertising and is never sold. Turning the permission off in Health Connect stops new reads, and deleting your account removes the stored nights.

Deleting your Drift account

In Drift, go to Settings → Account → Delete my account. That removes everything on the server and on that phone. Backups in Google Drive are files in your own Drive, so they stay until you delete them yourself. Or email bearzpu@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, naming Drift, and the account will be deleted.
