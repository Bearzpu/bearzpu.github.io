Privacy policy

Unmapped Streets is a private tool for a small delivery team. This page explains what it stores and why.

What we store

  • Your sign-in email, so you can log in. Accounts are created by the owner; there's no public sign-up.
  • Street records you save: street name, suburb, map position, notes and an optional photo.

Google Drive

Street photos are stored in the app owner's own Google Drive. The app only has access to the files it creates itself. It can't see, read or change anything else in that Drive, and it never accesses the Google account of anyone else who uses the app.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers or used for anything other than running the app. Photos are viewable by anyone who has their link, so the app can display them.

Removing your data

Ask the app owner and your account and anything you've added will be deleted.
